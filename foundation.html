A Founder’s Legacy

Honoring the life and service of Mary Ja Ne Williams, a faith-led builder of people, purpose, and dignified care.

Mary Ja Ne Williams

Founder • Educator • Community Advocate

Her Story

In 1985, Mary Ja Ne Williams earned her Bachelor of Arts degree in Business Administration, laying a strong foundation for a life devoted to service. Guided by unwavering faith and a deep commitment to her community, she founded Choo Choo Train Christian Academy in 1995.

What began with just five children grew under her dedicated leadership into a thriving educational community serving more than 300 students, including the development of a charter school, a youth camp, and a Head Start program that created opportunity and stability for families who needed it most.

Mary’s service expanded beyond education into community leadership and advocacy for youth, families, and seniors. Answering what she believed was a divine call, she established Healing H.A.D.C., Inc., a comprehensive 24/7 adult care facility rooted in compassion, dignity, and consistent support. Today, her legacy lives on through the people she helped, the programs she built, and the standard of care she set.

“A life of faith isn’t measured by words — it’s proven by what you build for others.”

A Living Legacy

Signature programs designed to uplift, educate, and strengthen the communities she served.

A dynamic one-week camp for teens and adults designed to educate, empower, and inspire through mental wellness, cancer awareness, nutritional guidance, and holistic personal development activities.

Awards for students in Galveston and surrounding areas pursuing careers in healthcare, behavioral health, or social work — building pathways for future community-focused professionals and compassionate leaders.

An annual gala event celebrating community partners, awarding student scholarships, and presenting the Distinguished Community Healing Award to those making transformative local and regional impact.
