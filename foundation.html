Mary Ja Ne Williams
A heart for people. A life of service. A legacy of hope.

Her Story

Mary Ja Ne Williams was the kind of woman who didn’t just believe in care — she lived it. She served with strength and tenderness, holding people up when life tried to push them down. Her life was marked by faith, dignity, and a deep commitment to helping others stand on their feet again.

Through her work supporting adults with disabilities and seniors, she created environments where people felt safe, valued, and seen — not as “cases,” but as human beings deserving of respect. This foundation carries her name because her love was not seasonal. It was consistent. It was intentional. It was legacy.

What We Do

We invest in people — because that’s what Mary did. Our work focuses on building pathways to stability, education, and wellness through programs that are practical, compassionate, and community-centered.

Legacy Scholarships

Scholarships and support for students entering medical, social, and behavioral health fields.

Hearts of Hope

Community initiatives that strengthen families and support vulnerable adults with dignity.

Seasonal outreach focused on wellness, education, and community connection.
