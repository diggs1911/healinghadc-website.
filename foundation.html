Mary Williams Foundation for Hope & Healing

Honoring a life of service by investing in people — scholarships, community support, and pathways that restore dignity, strengthen families, and expand hope.

Mary Williams

Founder • Servant-Leader • Builder of Community

Mary Williams lived with purpose and generosity — serving families with compassion, advocating for the vulnerable, and creating spaces where people could feel safe, seen, and supported.

This foundation carries her legacy forward through practical help and future-focused opportunity: support for children and families, scholarships for emerging caregivers and community leaders, and initiatives that strengthen wellness and stability.

“Legacy isn’t what we leave behind — it’s who we lift while we’re here.”
