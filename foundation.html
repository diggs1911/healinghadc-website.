A Living Legacy

The Mary Williams Foundation for Hope and Healing exists to honor a life devoted to faith, education, and dignified care, building pathways that uplift families and strengthen communities.

Mary Ja Ne Williams

Founder • Educator • Community Builder • Care Advocate

Her Story

In 1985, Mary Ja Ne Williams earned her Bachelor of Arts in Business Administration, laying a foundation for a life defined by service, faith, and community impact. Guided by unwavering purpose, she founded Choo Choo Train Christian Academy in 1995.

What began with five children grew into a thriving educational community serving over 300 students, and expanded into a charter school, youth camp, and a Head Start program that strengthened families and shaped futures.

Her commitment extended beyond education into advocacy for youth, seniors, and families navigating hardship. Answering a divine call to care for adults with dignity, Mary established Healing H.A.D.C., Inc., a 24/7 adult care home rooted in compassion, stability, and respect for the whole person.

Healing H.A.D.C., Inc. stands as a living testament to her love serving with dignity, compassion, and excellence.

Living Legacy Programs

Signature initiatives designed to educate, empower, and celebrate community impact, reflecting the values Mary lived.

A dynamic one-week camp for teens and adults designed to educate, empower, and inspire through mental wellness, cancer awareness, nutritional guidance, and holistic personal development activities.

Awards for students in Galveston and surrounding areas pursuing careers in healthcare, behavioral health, or social work building pathways for future community-focused professionals and compassionate leaders.

An annual gala event celebrating community partners, awarding student scholarships, and presenting the Distinguished Community Healing Award to those making a transformative local and regional impact.
