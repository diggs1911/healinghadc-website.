About Caring Hearts

At Caring Hearts Independent Living, we provide a warm, community-focused environment for adults needing daily support with activities of living. Our compassionate caregivers are onsite each day, and we partner with doctors and nurse practitioners—assigned according to each resident’s healthcare plan—to ensure comprehensive medical oversight.

Residents share comfortable suites and communal living spaces, enjoy meals prepared under the Texas Department of Agriculture’s Adult Food Program guidelines, and participate in engaging wellness and social programs designed to foster connection and joy.

Our Core Services

Nutrition & Meal Services

Balanced, home-style meals and nutrition support tailored to individual needs, preferences, and wellness goals—served in a safe, supportive environment.

Therapeutic Services

Supportive therapeutic resources and structured wellness activities that encourage emotional well-being, confidence, and connection.

Nursing & Health Monitoring

Medication support and ongoing health monitoring with licensed nursing oversight, plus coordination with providers as needed.

Transportation Services

Reliable transportation support for appointments, errands, and community activities—helping residents maintain independence.
