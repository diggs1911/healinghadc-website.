Caring Hearts Independent Living

Compassionate caregivers onsite daily, with doctors and nurse practitioners engaged per your personalized healthcare plan.

About Caring Hearts

At Caring Hearts Independent Living, we provide a warm, community-focused environment for adults needing daily support with activities of living. Our compassionate caregivers are onsite each day, and we partner with doctors and nurse practitioners—assigned according to each resident’s healthcare plan—to ensure comprehensive medical oversight.

Residents share comfortable suites and communal living spaces, enjoy meals prepared under the Texas Department of Agriculture’s Adult Food Program guidelines, and participate in engaging wellness and social programs designed to foster connection and joy.

Our Core Services

Nutritious Meal Service

Delicious meals prepared daily in accordance with the Texas Dept. of Agriculture Adult Food Program standards.

Therapeutic Activities

Weekly art and music sessions, exercise classes, and wellness workshops led by trained facilitators.

Nursing & Health Monitoring

Licensed nurses provide medication administration and health monitoring, with doctors & NPs consulted per plan.

Transportation Assistance

Coordinated rides for medical appointments, community events, and family visits.
