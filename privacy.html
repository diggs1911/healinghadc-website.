Privacy

How we handle information collected through our website.

Summary

This website intake is designed to avoid clinical PHI. Hospitals and providers send clinical records through established channels such as fax or encrypted transfer.

Information we collect

  • Contact and coordination information for referral and placement screening
  • Basic insurance details needed to support intake coordination
  • Legal status indicators and related document uploads when provided
  • Document uploads such as government ID and insurance cards

Information we do not request on the website

  • Clinical records, diagnosis notes, or detailed medical history
  • Full Social Security Number (only last four if provided)

Third-party services

Referral submissions route through Formspree based on the endpoint used on the referral page. Hosting is provided through GitHub Pages.

For questions about this privacy statement, use the contact page.
