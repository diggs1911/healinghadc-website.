Accessibility

Commitment to accessible experiences aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA.

Standards

We aim to support WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility practices across navigation, forms, headings, and interaction states.

Supported features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls
  • Visible focus states
  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Form labels and accessible grouping

Need assistance

If you experience accessibility issues, contact: info@healinghadc.org
